The 5 Navigation Areas

🗺️ Map
The relationship graph — the homepage. Shows all use cases and their connections to models, owners, risks, and mitigations. The living state of your org's AI.
🤖 Discover
Launch AI agent campaigns. Watch agents interview employees via Slack, email, or web. See use cases flow in as agents extract and validate data. The key differentiator.
📋 Register
Filterable table of all use cases with risk scores, ownership status, compliance coverage. Drill into any use case for full detail. Export for audits.
👤 Owners
People directory. Each person sees their AI use cases, pending risk assessments, and adoption tasks. Owners get notified and guided — not just named.
✅ Comply
Framework coverage dashboard: EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, OWASP LLM Top 10, ISO 42001. Shows which use cases have gaps, what's needed to close them. One-click gap remediation assignments.
